  • Requests send application/x-www-form-urlencoded payloads: x, y (in thousandths) and active.
  • The MSP430 clamps PWM to the safe window defined in motors.h. You can retune those constants without touching this page.
  • Store this file locally (or serve over HTTPS) and point it at your car's IP (default 192.168.4.1:8080).
  • Gamepad tuning lives in the script: adjust GAMEPAD_MAPPING for different button/axis slots and tweak the dead-zones (TURN_DEADBAND, ROTATION_DEADBAND, TRIGGER_DEADBAND) to taste.
